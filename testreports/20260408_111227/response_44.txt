**1. [优先] 为 Pod 添加对应的容忍度（Toleration）**

```yaml

  operator: "Equal"
  value: "true"
  effect: "NoSchedule"

然后应用更新：
```bash

kubectl taint nodes node1 test-unschedulable:true-

```
*依据*：消除调度障碍。
